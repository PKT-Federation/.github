# PKT Federation

**Evolving the human.**

PKT Federation is a collaborative team working across software development, systems architecture, learning design, and immersive environments. Our shared work explores how people learn, create, and interact with increasingly capable digital systems.

We bring together different disciplines to turn ideas into working software, prototypes, and tools. This organization is a home for that collective development effort.

## Shared work

Across the PKT/AMIA collaboration, our work connects intelligent systems, interactive worlds, and the infrastructure and authoring tools that support them.

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Intelligent systems and architecture** | Tools for building, connecting, and inspecting AI architectures, including OCA. |
| **Immersive worlds and authoring** | Spatial environments, narrative, and interaction through work such as RealityOS and Axis Mundi. |
| **Platform development** | The services, integrations, and development tools that support these connected experiences. |
| **Learning and human capability** | Learning design and interactive experiences that support exploration, understanding, and participation. |

These are connected areas of an evolving body of work. Public demonstrations include prototypes, development captures, and authoring tools; individual showcases identify their scope and contributors.

<summary>How these statistics are counted</summary>

The chart spans 2024-10-31 through 2026-10-28, showing all qualifying development history still reachable from the default and development branches of accessible private, non-fork organization repositories. The past-12-month totals cover 2025-10-29 through 2026-10-28. Retained history can predate the GitHub organization; deleted or otherwise unavailable history is not included.

Only aggregate counts are public. Code, private repository identities, and individual commit details remain confidential. Contributions across all author identities are included, including unlinked identities and AI-assisted work. Identical commits appearing on multiple branches or repositories count once. Dates use recorded committer timestamps in UTC; the current day is partial.

Public repositories, the private activity-automation repository, forks, configured imported upstream history, merge commits, and known GitHub Bot/[bot] authors are excluded. These statistics describe recorded activity, not hours worked, code quality, or an individual's share of the team's work. An automatic daily refresh is scheduled; the timestamp identifies the last successful snapshot. Failed collection or validation preserves the last verified snapshot. This is not a live GitHub contribution calendar. Color intensity uses quartiles of non-zero daily counts across the full retained period.

### Ken Knudsen
**President, CTO & Principal Architect | PKT Inc.**

Ken Knudsen is a software architect, debugging specialist, and systems innovator with over 30 years of experience designing, recovering, and modernizing complex enterprise software. His background spans banking, industrial systems, manufacturing, transportation, and broadcast media, including work with TD Bank, Invensys/Wonderware, and Harris/Imagine Communications. A published technical author, Ken specializes in resolving deeply rooted architectural problems and transforming complex, inherited systems into extensible software platforms.

His work in gamification and immersive systems reaches back to 1992, where he built and operated a long-running live-action gaming and simulation ecosystem incorporating persistent worlds, player progression, evolving narratives, rule-driven interactions, and community engagement. He later applied these principles to neurotechnology, developing gamified interfaces and supporting software infrastructure for a **start-ups stroke-survivor rehabilitation technology**. This experience continues to evolve through Axis Mundi, the Reality Generation Engine, immersive simulation development, and PKT's gaming/product prototypes used to validate AI-assisted software generation and delivery pipelines.

Today, Ken leads the architecture and development of **AMIA (Axis Mundi Intelligence Amplification)**, a governed AI development and agent infrastructure designed to transform architectural intent into controlled, test-validated software delivery. His work bridges sovereign and third-party AI systems, enterprise modernization, agent orchestration, canonical knowledge, simulation, and immersive application development—with an emphasis on reliability, human oversight, and building practical systems that expand what small teams can accomplish.
